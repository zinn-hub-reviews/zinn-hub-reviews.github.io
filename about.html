About Zinn Hub Review Sources

Zinn Hub Review Sources is a reference site about the numbers behind reviews of Zinn Hub, the freelance marketplace run by UK company Zinn Digital Ltd. It answers one kind of question: where does this figure come from, and what does it include?

Reviews of Zinn Hub appear in four places, each with its own rules, and the review site shows each list with a different star cut-off. Here we explain those cut-offs, quote the full Trustpilot and Google scores, and show how to compare like with like.

The site is published by Zinn Hub Reviews, part of Zinn Digital Ltd. Every figure is dated and linked to its original page.
